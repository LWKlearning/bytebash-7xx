In this readme I will explain some stuff about the kick generator.
I use the kick generator mainly for creating a punch and character layer for my kicks.
I do not recommend using an export of this directly in your mixes, it will most likely just not sound very good.
Use this generator as a starting point for your kicks.

Place a 1/4 note at around F4 on the keyboard, in my opinion this works best for this patch.
EQ and layer it with other sounds to make it more full and balanced.
I suggest at least adding a nice low-end in the tail and punch, to balance out the mid- and high-end frequencies.
But you may want to EQ more and add additional layers to make it more full.
Also play around with other FX like reverb, delay and more distortion in your FX chain!

Use every macro to tweak it to your liking:

Macro 1: TONE
This knob will change the tonality of your kick.
It will bottom out at 350 Hz and goes up to ~1100 Hz, if you want to go lower or higher, change the filter settings.

Marco 2: INTENSITY
With this knob you can change the intensity of the tone.
You can also completely cancel out the tone to make interesting non-tonal tails.
50% means that there is very little resonance.
With 0% you will sort of cancel out the resonance.
100% for your daily dose of resonant ear-rape.
I usually go for 50-70% here.

Macro 3: POGO
The POGO knob controls the pitch envelope in LFO2, which controls the envelope of the kick.
Set it to 0% to make nice gliding kicks, or higher for more constant kicks with shorter attacks.
LFO2 is currently set to Beat Sync for ease of use, if you want more control, switch that off.

Marco 4: DURATION
Turn this one to 0% if you only want to create punches. It cuts (most of the) the tail off.

Filter:
Play around with different filter shapes for different results.
Simply click through them with the arrow keys (<>) until you hear something you like.

FX:
Highly recommend switching some of these on and off and changing up distortion types for different results.

OSC A / OSC B:
Use different wavetables for other sounds! Make your kick wider by increasing the stereo or more detuning, go HAM here.

SUB:
Change the sub shape for more interesting shapes. It will drastically alter your tail.

I made it with an older version of Serum (v1.113), hopefully everyone can use it that way.
