The following non-destructive optimizations were (automatically) applied to all samples:
  - Trimmed all silence at the end (this saved ~35 MB of space)
  - Normalized kick samples to 0 dB that were quieter than 0 dB
  - Stereo samples that had identical left/right channels were downsampled to 1 channel

Some destructive optimizations were also applied to some samples:
  - Clipped some samples to 0 dB. This decision was made because we can reasonably assume the author
    would be listening to the sample through a clipped audio output, so (hopefully) the samples are
